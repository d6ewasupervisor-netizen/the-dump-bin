# EOD field guidance

Photo isolation is a software responsibility (Batch 4 stamp guard, Batch 5 session keying). Do **not** train leads to Reset or clear photos as a workaround.

## Before send (keep forever)

In preview, glance at the photos and recipients for **this** store and date. If anything looks like another store or yesterday’s work, stop and fix it before Send.

That human check is good practice, not a substitute for the session model.
