# EOD coupling and portability audit

Ratings describe the code as it exists, not whether a future extraction is desirable.

| Major module | Rating | Evidence and coupling points | Incremental portability boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Authentication and role display | MOSTLY GENERIC | The frontend waits for a Bearer-session auth gate and applies roles through generic `data-requires-role` DOM policy (`EOD/index.html:4417-4491`). Backend identity supports Cloudflare/session JWTs, but corporate allowlists and `KOMPASS_*_EMAILS` role mappings are deployment-specific (`eod-api/src/auth-middleware.js:1-24`, `eod-api/src/auth-middleware.js:49-77`, `eod-api/src/auth-middleware.js:122-159`). | Session exchange, authenticated fetch, `/api/me`, `requireAuth`, and DOM role visibility are reusable; allowlist, KOMPASS role mapping, and SAS day-confirm remain policies/adapters. |

## Overall portability conclusion

The strongest portable seams are the browser image editor, materials selection/viewer UI, department-signature wizard, guest-handoff transport, and SMS QR block (`EOD/index.html:6529-6877`, `EOD/eod-materials-browser.js:233-463`, `EOD/eod-dept-signatures.js:290-569`, `EOD/eod-guest-handoff.js:101-171`, `EOD/eod-sms-optin-qr.js:23-171`). The central orchestration remains tightly bound to SAS visit IDs, Fred Meyer store identity, Kompass category semantics, Retail Odyssey routing, and fixed PDF templates (`EOD/index.html:11179-12282`, `EOD/index.html:9696-9987`, `eod-api/src/shift-management.js:287-758`).

## Autonomous / provider-optional mode (standing product constraint)

See **`07-change-plan.md` → Standing constraint** and the full design in **`09-autonomy-schema.md`**. Owner may gate off SAS/PROD and/or Store Intelligence/Rebotics; timekeeping + set integrity continue via owned append-only snapshots and CSV set load (`eod-setlist` v1), with timesheet PDF independent of live providers. SAS is an importer into our schema, not the schema.
