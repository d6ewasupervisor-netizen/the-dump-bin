    EOD field app — instructions

    From sign-in to a sent EOD, in the order the visit runs.

    The field app is at the-dump-bin.com/eod-field-app. Use it on your work phone or tablet. Button names below are written exactly as they appear in the app.

    Revision 2026-11-11 · Field app 3.5.22

    1. Get into the Dump Bin

    One time only

    Before the first PIN can reach your phone, text JOIN to (509) 572-9212 from the mobile number on file with your supervisor. Until you do, no text can be delivered. Reply STOP anytime to opt out.

    1. Go to the-dump-bin.com. The sign-in page opens.
    2. In Email, enter your company email address — the one on file with your supervisor.
    3. Tap Text me a one-time PIN. A 6-digit PIN is texted to the phone number on record. It expires in about 10 minutes.
    4. Enter the PIN in 6-digit code and tap Verify & sign in.
    5. You are in. The sign-in lasts 45 days on that device.
    • No text arrived: text JOIN to (509) 572-9212 from that phone, wait a moment, then tap Resend code. Email me a sign-in link also works as a backup.
    • “You're not on the access list yet”: fill in your full name, email, and supervisor, then tap Request access. Sign in again once your supervisor confirms you were added.

    2. Open the field app

    1. On the Dump Bin home page, tap the EOD Field Pilot card.
    2. The phone bar along the bottom is Visit · Categories · Signatures · Send. Crew, Dump Bin, Helpdesk, and Scan results are under More. A tablet shows all of them on the bar.

    3. Start the shift — sign in to PROD

    The first time you open the app each day, the PROD + SI sign in sheet opens before store and date. If it was closed, open it again from PROD + SI sign in on Visit.

    1. Okta username is filled in as First.Last. Fix it only if it is wrong.
    2. Enter your Okta / SAS PROD password in Password.
    3. Finish with Okta Verify (preferred) or Google Authenticator, below.

    Okta Verify (preferred)

    1. Leave both code boxes blank.
    2. Tap Sign in. The sheet reads First Okta prompt sent. A second prompt follows.
    3. On your phone, open the Okta Verify notification and tap Yes, it's me.
    4. A second notification arrives. The sheet reads Second Okta prompt — approve it on your phone. Approve that one too.
    5. The sheet reads Approved, finishing… and then closes. The SAS and SI lights in the header turn green.
    Two prompts, every time

    The first prompt signs you in to Okta. The second signs you in to PROD. Approving only the first leaves you signed out. Do not close the sheet while you wait for the second prompt.

    Google Authenticator

    1. Open Google Authenticator and find the Okta entry.
    2. Type the 6-digit code into First code.
    3. Wait for the code to change. Codes roll over every 30 seconds.
    4. Type the new code into Second code.
    5. Tap Sign in.

    The app rejects one code by itself, or the same code in both boxes. Both codes are needed for the same reason Okta Verify sends two prompts.

    Set up Okta Verify (one time)

    1. Install Okta Verify from the App Store or Google Play on your work phone.
    2. In a browser, go to advantagesolutions.okta.com and sign in.
    3. Open the menu under your name and choose Settings.
    4. Under Security Methods, find Okta Verify and tap Set up.
    5. A QR code appears. In the Okta Verify app, tap + (add account) and scan it.
    6. Finish the prompts in the app. From now on, approve sign-ins with a tap instead of typing codes.

    If Okta Verify is not offered under Security Methods, contact your supervisor.

    Sign-in keeps failing

    After three failed tries the sheet closes and the visit opens anyway. Keep working — photos still save on the phone. Tell your supervisor so PROD sign-in can be fixed.

    4. Choose the store and day

    1. On Confirm store and date, tap Choose store and pick the store from the list. Search by number.
    2. The calendar opens on today. Days with a scheduled shift at that store are colored. Change the date only if the visit date is different.
    3. Tap Confirm.
    4. On Visit, the shift list loads. Your shift is selected for you, and the lead name and email fill in from it.
    • Not on the schedule: the app asks for an override. Tap Request override, give the reason, and wait for your supervisor to approve.
    • Unfinished visit from an earlier day: choose Resume to keep working it, or Fresh start to begin today.

    5. Kompass cart before photo and check-in manager

    Both are on Visit, below the shift list. Send stays locked until both are done.

    1. Under Kompass cart — before, tap Camera and photograph the cart before anything comes off it. The photo uploads to the Kompass maintenance set in PROD in the background.
    2. No cart at the store: tap No Kompass Cart and photograph the Kompass area or the Vestcom pickup area instead.
    3. Under Manager checked in with, type the name and title of the manager you checked in with. Tap a saved name chip or Choose saved name if they are already listed. Names are saved for that store.

    Already took the cart photo in PROD? Pull from PROD brings it into the app.

    6. Scan items

    The barcode button in the header opens Scan on every screen. Visit has Scan UPC and Categories has Scan as well.

    1. Point the camera at the barcode. If it will not read, type the last 4 or more digits of the UPC and tap Look up.
    2. Read the result card. It can take a few moments to fill in — the app checks this store's aisles, this store's sets, and the Kompass notes list at the same time. Be patient, or keep moving: every result is saved to Scan results under More.
      • Product photo, description, size, and the price at this store.
      • The aisle where this store carries it.
      • Set: — the reset the item belongs to. One item can show more than one set.
      • A banner when the item is on the Kompass notes list: New item · P##W# · this week, or Delete · P##W# · next week · tentative. A next-week banner means the item is for an upcoming reset. Hold it for that week.
    3. Tap the card to open that set's planogram and see exactly where the item goes.

    This item cannot be located at this time. means neither this store's aisle list nor this store's sets have the item. Set it aside and ask the store.

    Scanning the whole cart

    1. After the first read, the app asks Scan more items? Tap Yes.
    2. The camera stays open. Scan each item in turn.
    3. Tap Done Scanning. The results list groups every item by aisle, then by set.

    Scan results

    More → Scan results holds every item scanned during the visit — single scans and bulk scans — grouped by aisle, then by set. Tap an item to open its planogram. The list stays on the phone for that store and date, even if the app is closed. It clears when you confirm a different store or date.

    Store-specific

    Every lookup runs against the store and date you confirmed — that store's own aisle locations and that store's own set list in Store Intelligence. The same UPC can show a different aisle at another store. Confirm the store first, or Scan has nothing to match against.

    7. Categories — before and after photos

    Categories lists every set on today's sheet in aisle walk order. Each card shows the set name, DB key, aisle, size, version, estimated time, the category number (with the week it was issued under it), and a live PROD / SI status line.

    Filters along the top: Not Started, In Progress, Backlog, Complete, Not Executable, Out of Scope, Not in Prod. The shift buttons beside Refresh (ISE, Cut-In, Blitz, and so on) narrow the list to one shift.

    Before photos — start the set

    1. Before touching the set, tap Capture on its card.
    2. The camera opens on bay 1. Take one photo per bay. The camera moves to the next bay on its own (Moving to bay 2).
    3. After the last bay, the app returns to Categories. Photos upload in the background — keep working. The header shows how many are still syncing.
    4. The set moves to In Progress.

    Bay count follows the planogram size: one photo per 4 ft section (an 8 ft set is 2 photos), and one photo per door on cooler and freezer sets. The app sets the count. Follow it.

    After photos — finish the set

    1. When the set is done, tap Capture on the same card. It now takes after photos.
    2. Shoot each bay from the same spot as its before photo.
    3. The after photos upload to PROD and Store Intelligence. The app closes the set in both. There is no need to open SI.
    4. The status line reads PROD complete and SI complete, and the set moves to Complete.
    Stay on signal

    Photos are safe on the phone until they upload. Do not clear the browser or sign out with photos still syncing.

    8. Open the planogram for a set

    1. Tap the set card itself (not Capture or a mark button). The set opens.
    2. Tap Planogram. One bay shows at a time. Swipe left or right for the next bay.
    3. Tap any product for its details.
    4. Text / Photos switches between item descriptions and product pictures.
    5. The filter row shows only what changed: All, Changes, New, Moves, Facings, New UPC. A filter appears only when the planogram has that kind of change.

    From the same screen, Before and After show the photos for that set, one bay at a time, with zoom and rotate.

    9. Mark sets that will not be completed

    Every set must be Complete or carry a mark before the EOD can send. Mark buttons sit on each card: Not in Store, Not in SI, Backlog, Out of Scope, Not Executable.

    To mark several sets at once, tick their checkboxes (or select all above the list). A bar appears above the bottom nav with the same marks for every selected set.

    Out of Scope — another vendor's set

    1. Tap Out of Scope on the set.
    2. The app asks to remove it from the sign-off sheet as another project. Tap Remove.
    3. The set moves to the Out of Scope filter and drops off the printed sign-off. Nothing is written to PROD or SI.

    Marked by mistake? Tap the lit mark and choose Undo Out of Scope.

    Not in Store — and the help desk ticket

    1. Tap Not in Store on the set.
    2. The app asks Report this to the help desk?
      • Please Report opens the KOMPASS Help Desk Report with the set details already filled in. Add photos, then tap Submit reports.
      • Don't Report marks the set without a ticket.
    3. Either way, the app closes the set in PROD with the comment Not in store, closes it in Store Intelligence, and adds a Not in store: line to the EOD notes.

    Backlog — not worked today

    Tap Backlog on a set the crew will not reach today. It counts as handled for Send and carries to the next visit. The Backlog label clears on its own once a before photo is taken or the set is completed.

    Not Executable — needs a reason and a comment

    1. Tap Not Executable on the set.
    2. Pick a reason: Not In Store, Planogram Issue, Fixture Issue, or Other. The app will not save the mark without one.
    3. Write what is wrong in Comments. Write one every time. The app refuses Other with no comment.
    4. Tap Please Report to open the help desk report pre-filled from your reason and comment, or Don't Report.

    The reason and comment go into the PROD comment on that set and into the EOD notes. Once the EOD sends, your supervisor gets a separate notice listing each Not Executable set. The set stays on the sign-off sheet.

    Not in SI

    A flag only. The set is in PROD but has no Store Intelligence task. It still needs to be completed, or marked Backlog or another mark, before Send.

    10. Help desk

    For any problem not covered by a mark — wrong version, missing fixture or hardware, reverse flow, an obstruction — use the help desk report directly.

    1. Open More → Helpdesk, then Open help desk report.
    2. Choose the Issue type.
    3. Pick the Shift and Set. If the set is not on your shift, tick Set isn't on my shift and enter the set name, category number, version, DB key, and footage.
    4. Fill in Details — location, measurements, anything the help desk needs.
    5. Add photos with Take photo or Choose photos. Photos are strongly recommended.
    6. More than one problem? Tick Report additional issues and add the next one.
    7. Add Retail Odyssey Team is on by default. Add any other CC, then tap Submit reports. Each issue goes out as its own email.

    11. Collect department signatures

    Signatures lists only the departments worked on today's sheet — for example Grocery, Produce, Bakery, Deli, Meat, Home. Each department manager signs for their own sets, every visit. Collect them while those managers are still on the floor.

    Way 1 — hand them the phone

    1. Tap Hand to signer on the department.
    2. The manager picks their name from the list, or Someone new.
    3. A new signer enters full name, email, an optional cell number, and title. The email is saved for that store and copied on the EOD. The cell number lets you text them a link next visit.
    4. They review today's sets (tap a set for its before and after photos), or skip to the signature.
    5. They sign and tap Save signature.

    Way 2 — Daily PIC QR

    1. At the top of Signatures, tap Show QR.
    2. The manager scans it with their own phone camera, opens the sign-out page, and signs for their department on their phone.

    Way 3 — text the manager a link

    1. Tap Text PIC.
    2. Pick the manager. They receive a link and sign on their own phone.

    Text PIC only lists managers who left a cell number on an earlier signature and have texted JOIN to (509) 572-9212. No opted-in numbers yet means use the phone or the QR.

    A collected line shows Signed by with the name and email. Edit fixes a typo in the name or email, Clear removes it, and Re-collect takes it again.

    12. Crew — distribute time across the sets

    Do this once every person on the shift has clocked out and every set is Complete or marked.

    1. Open More → Crew.
    2. Tap Distribute time. The app splits each person's punched time across the sets they worked and writes it to PROD.
    3. Read the result:
      • Time distributed — time per shift type and a total by person count, ending in Distributed and verified against PROD. Done.
      • Not ready to distribute — lists sets that still need Complete or an exception mark, and punches that still need to be finalized or marked No Show. Fix those (Open in PROD goes straight there), then run it again.
      • Lead has zero hours — the lead's own punch has no time. Exit to fix the punch first, or Continue if that is correct.
      • Time did not match — names each person whose punch time and set time disagree. Correct the punch, then run it again.

    InstaWork workers have their own Distribute time button in the InstaWork section of Crew.

    13. Sign and send the EOD

    1. Open Send. Day summary shows send ready or open sets remain.
    2. Under Kompass cart — after, tap Camera and photograph the cart at the end of the day.
    3. Read Notes. Not in Store and Not Executable lines are already there. Add anything else the store or your supervisor needs to know.
    4. Under Lead signature, tap Sign and sign.
    5. Check the recipients — see below.
    6. Clear every remaining item — see below.
    7. Tap Preview. Confirm the store number, date, photos, and recipients all belong to this visit.
    8. Tap Send EOD. EOD sent! confirms it. Tap Start new visit when you move to the next store.

    Verify the addresses

    • Store addresses already saved for that store show as checkboxes. Tick each one that should receive this EOD. Untick anyone who has left the store.
    • To add a store contact, type their @stores.fredmeyer.com address in Add recipient and tap Add. Only store addresses are accepted. A small typo in the store domain is corrected for you — check the corrected address is right.
    • On Send EOD, the app asks to confirm how many store contacts will receive it. If it says none are selected, go back and tick them.
    • A saved contact may show Keep / Remove from store. Choose one for each. Send stays locked until you do.
    • Department managers who signed are copied on the EOD with the email they entered.
