What this is

The Store Survey captures how each Fred Meyer in Division 701 actually works — Kompass cart, supplies, staging, Vestcom, contacts, and close-out. You do one store at a time. Answers save as you go. Every question is optional. When you submit, that visit is locked on record. A later retake adds a new response; it does not erase the old one.

1 Sign in

Use the same Dump Bin sign-in you already use for EOD and other tools. One login covers the survey.

1 Open https://the-dump-bin.com on your phone or tablet.
2 Enter the work email your supervisor has on file.
3 Tap Email me a sign-in link and open the email on that same device.
Text code instead
Tap Text me a one-time PIN. Codes go to the mobile number on the survey roster. If you have never opted in, text JOIN to (509) 572-9212 first, then request the PIN again.

2 Open the survey

1 On the Dump Bin home page, tap Store Survey.
2 Or go straight to https://the-dump-bin.com/survey/.

If you were emailed an invite, that link signs you in and can open a specific store for you.

Display
Use A+ / A− to change text size, and Dark to cycle light / dark / inverse. Those settings stay on this device.

3 Pick your store

The home screen is split into three lists. You only survey one store at a time.

Section What it shows What you do
Today's stores Stores from today's Kompass ISE schedule that you have not submitted yet. Your current store is listed first. Tap the store, then tap Open store at the bottom.
Your week Each remaining day this week and the store(s) on that day. Use it to plan. Open a store when you are actually there.
In progress & completed Drafts you started and stores you already submitted. Tap a row to review, finish a draft, or retake a submitted store.

Store is not on today's list

1 Under Change / pick a store, choose the district.
2 Choose the store, then tap Use store.
3 Tap Open store.
Already completed
After you submit, that store leaves Today's stores and moves to In progress & completed. You can still open it. You will be asked to confirm before opening a completed store.

4 Answer questions

Questions come one at a time, sections A through K (cart, supplies, staging, Vestcom, contacts, close-out, and so on). The store name is filled into the question for you.

Skip anything

Every question is optional. Tap Next if you do not know or prefer not to answer.

Saves as you go

Look at the top right for Saved ✓. You can leave, switch stores, or lose signal — drafts come back.

Yes / No auto-advance

Tapping a single choice moves you to the next question. Multi-select stays put until you tap Next.

Comments & photos

Every question has Add a comment. Some also have a camera. Photos are optional and appreciated.

Useful buttons

  • Common: … — a frequent answer from other stores. Tap it only if it matches this store.
  • ⌂ Home — back to the store list. Your draft is saved first.
  • Back — previous question. Nothing is lost.
  • 📷 — take or attach a photo. It is resized on the phone before upload.
Tip
If the header says Offline — retrying, keep answering. The app retries on its own when the connection returns.

5 Review and submit

1 After the last question, you land on Review. You can also tap Review & submit from the answers list.
2 Tap any row to change that answer. Blanks are allowed.
3 Tap Submit store at the bottom.
Submit locks this visit
After submit, those answers cannot be edited. They stay on record for the division. If you need to add another pass, use Retake survey — that starts a new submission and leaves the old one alone.

6 After you submit

  • You will see a confirmation for that store.
  • Tap Home to do the next store on today's list.
  • Tap View answers to read what you sent (read-only).
  • Tap Retake survey only if you need a second submission for the same store.

7 If you are management

Supervisors and managers take the same survey as reps. Use this page when you are in a store. Use Survey Admin when you are assigning coverage or reading results.

  • Your Today's stores list still follows the live Kompass ISE schedule when your name is on it.
  • You can also pick any store in the catalog with District → Store if you are covering or checking a location.
  • Submitting as yourself counts as a real response for that store. Do not retake unless you mean to add another visit.
  • To assign other people, cancel assignments, or export answers, open Survey Admin. Step-by-step for that tool: Survey Admin guide.
Two different pages
Store Survey = fill it out. Survey Admin = who is covering which store, and what they answered. Supervisors see the stores they supervise. Managers and directors see the full division.

8 If something goes wrong

What you see What to do
Not on survey roster Ask your supervisor to add your work email to the roster, then sign in again with that email.
No store on today's schedule Pick district and store under Change / pick a store. Schedule comes from live SAS PROD when it is available.
Offline — retrying Keep going. Walk toward better signal if you can. Drafts are also kept on the phone until they sync.
Already submitted — locked That visit is on record. Use Retake only to add a new submission.
Text PIN never arrives Text JOIN to (509) 572-9212 from that phone, wait a minute, then tap Resend — or use the email sign-in link.
