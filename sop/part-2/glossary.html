Glossary

    Part II — Glossary

    The ones you'll hear in your first week

    Learn these eleven and most conversations will make sense. Everything else you can look up.

    TermShort version
    KompassThe program you work — Kroger Optimization Management Plan Aligning Store Sales
    POGPlanogram. The map for your assignment
    BayEach 4-foot segment of a gondola, numbered left to right
    SetA grouping of like items — pickles, peanut butter, laundry detergent. Also called a commodity
    FacingHow many of one product sit side by side across the front of the shelf
    Data skinThe clear channel that snaps onto the shelf front and holds the strip
    StripThe printed label strip that slides into the data skin
    TagThe individual price label for one product. Technically a UPL
    BackstockProduct that won't fit on the shelf. Goes to top stock, a U-boat, or a banana box — never left in the aisle
    DIDiscontinued item — comes off the set, carries a closeout tag
    NIINew item introduction — a partial project adding new products to an existing set
