Kompass Commodities

    Part II — Kompass Commodities

    A commodity is a grouping of like items — pickles, laundry detergent, frozen pizza. Same thing this document calls a set, seen from the store's side rather than the shelf's.

    Every commodity has a number, and that number appears on the shelf strip and in the planogram code. Sections 7.1 and 7.7 cover where to find it.

    This is the Kompass list. These are the categories the program covers. A commodity that isn't here generally isn't part of this program — useful to know before you go hunting for a planogram that was never going to exist.

    Two ways to use this: the department view shows what's in scope for a department. The numeric index goes the other way — read a number off a strip, find out what it is.

    Recent change: Pet moved to Department 03

    All Pet planogram names now read Department 03 instead of Department 01, following a change to the family tree.

    Ten commodities are affected — wet and dry dog food, dog treats, wet and dry cat food, cat treats, cat litter, wild bird and animal feed, frozen and refrigerated pet food, and pet care supplies.

    What this means at the shelf: a pet planogram code now carries D03 where it used to carry D01. Older paperwork, older planogram printouts, and anyone's memory will still say 01. The set hasn't moved and the work hasn't changed — only the department it files under. Marked ‡ in the list below.
