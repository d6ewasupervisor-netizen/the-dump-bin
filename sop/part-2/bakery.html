Bakery

    Part II — Bakery

    Read this before your first bakery reset, and keep it with you on visit day. Bakery is a Kompass reset like any other, and almost none of the mechanics are the same.

    What's different:

    • The planogram is read differently, and it lives on the table rather than in your hand
    • Black ID tags go under the product instead of shelf strips in front of it
    • Magnetic shelf strip on most refrigerated cases, so channel guides have something to stick to
    • POG books installed on a clear hook and plastic sleeve for every set without shelf strips — tables, artisan wall, refrigerated and frozen bunkers
    • No substitutions, ever. A facing with no product stays blank
    • Fifteen minutes out of the case, not twenty. Bakery product is delicate and melts fast

    That last one is the biggest departure from the rest of this document. In grocery, an out-of-stock facing gets a dummy facing to hold the space. In bakery it stays empty. Don't carry the grocery habit across.

    Getting started

    1. Check in at the front desk so the store knows you're on site and starting the bakery reset
    2. Find the Kompass cart in the back room. It should hold your materials, tools, and the bakery planogram
    3. Locate the Black ID tags. They ship with the shelf tags, but depending on the store they may be somewhere else. If you can't find them, ask the store's File Maintenance Coordinator or store management — they're usually with batches of price tags
    4. Take a before photo of the set or table you're about to start

    Tiered tables usually need more than one before photo — one of each side, front and back, to capture everything on it.

    Reading the bakery planogram

    The front page tells you which set it covers — Breakfast, Ambient Cupcakes, Refrigerated Case — shows product images for each facing, and lists fixture dimensions so you can confirm you have the right set.

    The back of the first page is the Add/Delete page. It tells you what to remove and what to ask the bakery team for.

    Orientation: read the planogram standing with your back to the front of the store. Table 1 is the one closest to you. Table 5 is the furthest away.

    The remaining pages number every product by table and position.

    Column What it means
    FaceHow many facings the table needs of that product
    Face DA suggestion only. The division dictates stack height — follow division direction, not this number

    Face D is the one to watch. Everywhere else in this document the planogram is the authority; here one column isn't.

    Black ID tags

    Every product on the bakery tables and the artisan bread wall gets a black ID tag.

    Event type What the store receives
    Kompass UpdateA tag for every facing — an item with 4 facings gets 4 tags
    NII or maintenanceTags only for the items that are changing

    The rules

    • Remove all old tags first, then wipe the table free of dust and debris
    • Every product gets a fresh tag — even one that already has a tag and isn't moving
    • Never stack a new tag on an old one. The old one comes off first, always

    Placement

    Centered under the product and fully hidden, oriented the way the product faces. Horizontal product, horizontal tag and text. Vertical product, vertical tag and text.

    When black ID tags are missing

    Make a temporary tag. Never skip a facing.

    With the handheld and tag printer:

    1. Put a blank black ID tag in the facing position
    2. Print an item label with the description and UPC
    3. Apply the white label on top of the blank black tag

    Without access to the handheld and printer:

    1. Put a blank black ID tag in the facing position
    2. Affix a Post-it with that item's UPC on top of it

    That lets the store print the white label on their Zebra and apply it over the black tag. Older paperwork calls that device a Telxon or Telson — same thing.

    Bakery tables

    1. Remove all deleted items using the delete page. Once you've gathered them, find someone in the bakery department and let them tell you where to put them
    2. Remove all remaining product and all old black ID tags, then wipe the table clean
    3. Place the new black ID tags following the numbered guide on page 2 of the planogram

    Reading direction changes partway across. Left to right for Tables 1, 2, and the front half of Table 3. Right to left for the back half of Table 3 and Tables 4–5.

    • Center each tag, oriented the way the product will face
    • Every facing gets a fresh tag, whether or not the product moved
    1. Put product back. If there are holes for new product, ask the bakery department to help you find it. If a new product isn't in the store, leave the facing blank — no substitute product, no improvising. That's the store's call, not yours
    2. Swap the POG book on the side of the table
    3. Take your after photos — again, more than one for a tiered table
    4. Capture the reset in the Store Intelligence app for Kompass compliance

    The POG book

    • Remove and dispose of the previous book, slide the new POG into the plastic sleeve, put it back on the hook
    • Check with your Deli/Bakery Merchandiser on whether they want POG books installed for additional sets
    • Hook and sleeve should be available after the stand-up process, and the set team should have spares
    • Missing hook or sleeve? Notify store management and the bakery team so they can pull a replacement from the school and hardware section
    • No sleeve at all — hand the planogram directly to a bakery team member. Never leave it loose

    Refrigerated and frozen

    1. Pull all the old strips from the current set
    2. Pull the deleted items using the add/delete page, and have a bakery team member direct you where they go. Refrigerated items go to refrigerated backstock, frozen to frozen backstock — so nothing gets put away wrong
    3. Remove everything and clean the shelves thoroughly. Check whether the black magnetic strips or channel guides need replacing — magnetic black tape is part of the supplies for refrigerated sets. If channels are missing and you don't have what you need to fix it, contact me

    Food safety: 15 minutes in bakery — not the 20 you get everywhere else. Refrigerated and frozen bakery product is never out of the case longer than fifteen minutes. Bakery items are delicate and they melt fast. Frosting slumps, cream and mousse soften, and glazes go tacky well before a case of frozen vegetables would even start to sweat. Damage here shows on the product, and a customer can see it. Stage your work so product goes back quickly. Pull what you can put back.

    1. Cut the new shelf strips along the dotted line to fit. Refrigerated shelves usually run 56 inches to 5 feet, and strips only come in 3-foot and 4-foot increments — expect multiple bays and spare strips

    Channel guides go in with the open edge facing down, so crumbs can't fill the channel. Guides first, then the strips into the guides.

    1. Replace the strips and hang all new tags provided
    2. Move product back to match the new strips, then fill holes with new product. Ask bakery where the new items are. Not in store? Facing stays blank
    3. After photos, then capture in Store Intelligence

    Bunkers and mobile cases — M1, Q2, SIM

    Mirrors the table process, with one difference: the tags mount on magnetic tape.

    This covers the M1 bunker and the Q2 and SIM mobile cases. Same procedure for all three.

    1. Remove deleted items, and have bakery direct you where they go
    2. Remove all remaining product and all black ID tags, then wipe the bunker clean
    3. Use the guide on page 4 of the POG and follow the numbered products left to right. Magnetic tape should be on hand — cut it to the size of each black ID tag and stick the tag to the tape
    • - Center each tag, oriented the way the product faces
    • - Every facing gets a fresh tag
    • - If the case won't take magnetic tape, use double-sided sticky tape instead. Not every bunker or mobile case is magnetic-compatible, and the tag still has to go on
    1. Put product back. Holes for new product — ask bakery. Not in store — leave the facing blank

    The 15-minute bakery rule applies here too. The bunker holds refrigerated and frozen product, and it's delicate for the same reasons. Work in stages so nothing sits out.

    Artisan bread wall

    1. Remove all product and all black ID tags
    2. Pull the deleted items using the add/delete page, and have bakery direct you where they go
    3. Wipe the shelves down
    4. Add the black ID tags following the planogram
    5. Move product back to match, then fill holes with new product

    No shelf strips and no sale tags on the artisan wall — black ID tags only. Everything is priced as marked, and prices vary by size.

    1. After photos, then capture in Store Intelligence

    No substitutions. Facing stays blank if the product isn't in store.

    Racks and baskets

    Covers the Colossal Cookie Rack, 3-tier baskets, and the other racks in the department.

    1. You'll get a planogram guide for any bakery change on any rack
    2. Find the right rack, pull all product, and remove all black ID tags from the back of the rack. Clean off dust and debris
    3. Place the new black ID tags along the back of the rack, following the planogram

    Two things are different here. Tags go on the back of the rack, not the front. And each UPC gets one tag — not one per facing, the way tables work.

    1. Add product to the rack per the planogram
    2. After photos, then capture in Store Intelligence

    Packaged bread destination

    1. Remove all product, and remove the strips, tags, and channels
    2. Pull the deleted items using the add/delete page, and have bakery direct you where they go
    3. Wipe the shelves down
    4. Add the new shelf strips and channels per the planogram
    5. Put product back to match the new strips, then fill holes with new product. Not in store — facing stays blank

    The display has four sides. Bay 1 is Bakery Facing — the side facing the bakery tables. Bays 2 through 4 continue left to right: Front End, Outside, Back End.

    1. After photos, then capture in Store Intelligence

    The Kompass Bakery Completion Standard

    The bakery team supports us — finding product, telling us where deletes go. Don't expect them to complete any part of the reset. The reset is ours, start to finish.

    Before you leave the store, all six of these are done:
    1The reset is 100% complete. Blank facings stay blank where product is unavailable — never substitutions
    2Fresh black ID tags on every facing. Missing tags follow the temporary tag process — never skip a facing
    3All materials handled. Unused labels and the old POG handed to a bakery team member — nothing left behind
    4The POG book is swapped on every set, and any missing hook or sleeve is replaced from the school and hardware section
    5After photos taken and uploaded to Store Intelligence — every side, every set
    6Walk the completed set before you leave — reps with their lead, leads with the store. Section 9.1

    If you can't finish

    Contact me before you leave the store — not after. We'll make a plan together.
