    Before you touch product — 8.2

    Version · Effective date · Notes · Size · Shelf count · New shelves · Strip count · Boxes staged · New stock located

    Size code: F feet · D doors · I inches. Read the letter before you reach for the tape.

    Footage or shelf count doesn't match → stop. Tell your lead before you dismantle anything.

    Count every shelf, including empty ones and signage shelves.

    Count your strips against the planogram before you pull any. Full reset → a strip for every position. Partial or NII → strips only for what's changing, so pull only what you can replace.

    The working order — 8.3

    Bay 1 → right. Bottom shelf → up. Position 1 → right.

    Shelves and pegs on the same set: shelves bottom-up first, then pegs top-down.

    Measuring — 8.4, 7.3

    Top of shelf → top of the next shelf up.

    • Read the tape at eye level, head-on
    • Won't sit exactly? Leave too much space, not too little
    • Bread and chips — wire shelves. Measure to where the product sits, not the front lip
    • Cross-check the top shelf against DISTANCE FROM BASE SHELF on the strip — red, small type

    Pegboard holes are 1 inch apart, center to center, both directions. Frog: left leg in the specified hole.

    When it doesn't fit — 7.6
    Multiple facings won't all fitTake it down by one facing. Keep working
    No room for even one facingBorrow a facing from a neighbor with several. Keep working
    Footage, shelf count, version or format wrongStop. Tell your lead

    Both fitting cases: call it out to your lead. Neither stops the set.

    Product not in stock: dummy facing — same-size item turned backwards. Bakery is the exception: the facing stays blank.

    Tagging — 8.4 Step 8

    No tag on a position? Strip present → the strip stands in. No strip → hand-write one. Either way: highlight it on the planogram and hand the planogram to your lead.

    DSL stores

    Order: rail → channel → strip → tag. Never a tag before a strip.

    Size Where
    1.5Tight barcode space
    2.6Most of the store
    4.2 / 4.5Produce bins, large format
    MagnetCoolers only

    Size is printed on the strip. Covers another barcode → size DOWN.

    Associate: open DSL app → scan tag → scan strip → wait for the flash → snap it in from the bottom, listen for it.

    Take the paper tag down only after the flash.

    End of set: disassociate every delete. Leftovers break re-association. Unused tags and removal tools back on the Kompass cart.

    Not currently installed in: freezers, frozen, GM, floral, service departments, fuel center, freestanding displays, wine, tobacco, seasonal, American Greetings, gift cards, clip strips, lobby, dump bins, furniture. GM stays on UPLs.

    The five box labels — Part II, Storage

    Backstock · Discontinued · Not In Set · Damaged · Expired

    Sort into them as you pull and the labeling takes care of itself. Label as you fill, not at the end.

    Boxed product: leave it with the receiver if they can take it soon. If not, palletize it.

    Cold product

    20 minutes out of the case. Bakery is 15.

    Watch which one you put it back in — refrigerated into a freezer is a write-off, and so is the reverse.

    Before you leave — Section 9

    Walk the set. Rep → with your lead. Lead → with the store.

    • Aisle clear — no carts, totes, trays, crates
    • Pull categories delivered, trash gone, spills cleaned
    • What you worked, conditioned. Full reset: the whole set. Partial: your area
    • Every cart emptied and returned to the corral
    • Borrowed equipment back to the receiver
    • After photos, submitted in Store Intelligence — fix what it flags before you move on
    • Clock out in the Field App

    Can't finish? Tell your lead before you leave. What's done, exactly where you stopped, what's blocking it, where the materials are.

    Things that are always true

    • The planogram is the authority. The shelf is real. Adjust facings, never redesign the set
    • Scan the UPC, not the description
    • Strip only what has to move. Height changing, product moving, shelf swapped, shelf edge being worked — otherwise leave it
    • The tag travels with the product
    • Older to the front, newer to the back
    • A loose shelf is in your hands or locked into the gondola. There is no third state
    • Nothing goes home with you
    • 18 or older, and shown that machine, before you touch a baler
