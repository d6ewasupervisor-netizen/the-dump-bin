SAS Automator update

This download works once. Save it on the USB stick, not the office computer.

  1. Plug in the USB stick.
  2. Chrome → chrome://settings/downloads → Change → pick the SAS Automator Portable folder on the USB stick.
  3. Come back here and tap Download update.
  4. On the USB stick, open the zip. Extract into that same USB folder. Replace existing files.
  5. chrome://extensions → reload SAS Retail Automator (Portable). Unlock with your pattern.
