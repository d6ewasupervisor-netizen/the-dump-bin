What this is

Survey Admin is the office side of the Division 701 store survey. Use it to see which stores still need someone, assign this week's Kompass ISE leads (or anyone else), read submitted answers, and export spreadsheets and photos. It does not replace taking the survey yourself — when you are in a store, use Store Survey the same way a rep does.

1 Who can use this

Role What you see
Retail supervisor Stores you supervise (and your home district). The blue banner says District view.
Manager, director, or division admin All Division 701 stores. The banner says Division view.
Rep / lead without supervisor access This page is blocked. They use Store Survey only. The Dump Bin home card for Survey Admin stays hidden.

You must be on the survey roster with a work email. Same Dump Bin sign-in as everyone else — no second password.

2 Open admin

1 Sign in at https://the-dump-bin.com.
2 On home, under Office & Admin, tap Survey Admin.
3 Or go to https://the-dump-bin.com/survey-admin/.

Two tabs at the top: Stores (coverage and assigning) and Results (answers and exports).

3 Stores tab — coverage

Each store has one status. Filter with the chips, the district dropdown, or the search box.

Status Meaning What you do
Needs assign Nobody is covering this store yet, and it is not submitted. Assign from PROD, or tap Assign on the row to pick a person.
Assigned Someone has an open assignment. They may not have submitted yet. Wait, remind them, or cancel and reassign.
Done At least one submitted survey exists for this cycle. Read it on Results. You can still assign a retake if you need another pass.
Phone vs desktop
On a phone you get store cards. On a wider screen you get the table. Same data either way. Tap Refresh after assigning.

4 Assign from PROD (weekly path)

This is the normal way to cover a week. It pulls live Kompass ISE leads from SAS PROD, matches them to the roster, and can email each person a magic-link invite.

1 On Stores, tap Assign from PROD (or open that panel).
2 Set Cycle to Current (this week), Next, a named cycle, or a single day.
3 Tap Sync with PROD. Wait until the status line shows visit counts and matched leads.
4 Skim the list. Roster means we found their email. Needs email means type one before you assign.
5 Optional: filter by district, team, or lead name. Use Select visible / Clear selection.
6 Leave Email invites when assigning checked unless you will tell people yourself.
7 Tap Assign selected. Coverage chips update. Invites go to the addresses on those rows.
Unmatched names
If a lead is on the PROD schedule but not on the roster, enter their work email on that row. The invite still works. Ask Tyson if a name should be added to the roster for next time.
Invite email override
Even matched leads have an Invite email box. Change it if the roster email is wrong for this person this week.

5 Assign someone else

Use this for a fill-in, an extra store, or anyone who is not on this week's PROD pull.

1 Open Assign someone else. (The Assign button on a Needs-assign row opens this with that store already checked.)
2 Search Who by name, or type a full email. Pick them from the list. Off-roster emails are allowed — they still get an invite.
3 Check the store(s). Filter the list if needed. Turn on Include stores already done only if you want a retake or a second response.
4 Leave Email a magic-link invite checked, then tap Assign.
Done stores
Assigning a store that is already Done does not wipe the old survey. It lets someone add another response. Confirm the prompt only if that is what you want.

6 Cancel an assignment

On the store row (or card), next to the person's name, tap Cancel and confirm. That assignment is dropped. The store goes back to Needs assign if nobody else is covering it and it is not already submitted.

7 Results — read answers

1 Tap the Results tab.
2 Filter by district, store, and status. Default is Submitted only. Choose Submitted + drafts if you need in-progress work.
3 Tap a store card. You get every answered question, comments, and photo thumbnails. Problem answers (not the “good” value) show in red.
4 Use Prev / Next (or the arrow keys on a keyboard) to walk stores. ← List goes back. Escape also closes the viewer.

You only see responses inside your scope. A D6 supervisor does not see D8 stores.

8 Export CSV, Excel, photos

Exports honor the filters currently set on Results (district, store, status).

Button What you get
Export CSV Spreadsheet of responses, including comment columns and optional photo links.
Export XLSX Excel workbook built in the browser from the same filtered set.
Photos ZIP All photos for the filtered responses, in one download.
Photo links expire
If Include photo links is checked, those URLs last about 30 days. Download the ZIP if you need a lasting copy.

9 Take the survey yourself

Admin does not fill out questions. When you are in a store:

1 Open https://the-dump-bin.com/survey/ (Store Survey on home).
2 Follow the same steps as a rep: pick the store, answer what you know, submit.

Full field steps, including skip/save/retake: Store Survey guide.

Your submission counts
A survey you submit as yourself is a real division response. Use Retake only when you intend a second visit on record.

10 If something goes wrong

What you see What to do
Survey admin is for supervisors & managers Your account is not in supervisor/manager scope. Confirm the roster role and that supervisor-store rows exist. Reps cannot open this page.
Could not load PROD / Sync fails Try Sync with PROD again. If it keeps failing, the SAS PROD session on the API may need a refresh — ping Tyson.
Lead shows Needs email Type their work email on that row, then assign. Do not leave it blank.
Invite says failed Assignment can still exist. Copy the survey link and send it yourself, or re-assign with a corrected email.
No responses on Results Check filters (Submitted vs drafts, district). People may still be in Assigned, not Done.
Wrong store assigned Cancel that assignment, then assign the correct person/store.
