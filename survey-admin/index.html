How it works

  1. Sync pulls live Kompass ISE shifts from SAS PROD.
  2. Filter by district, team, or lead name — then check who to assign.
  3. Roster-matched leads use their system email; unmatched leads need a manual email.
  4. Assignments cover each lead’s scheduled stores in the selected window.

Or walk through a manual assignment: who should take the survey, which stores they cover, when it’s due, then send magic-link invites.
