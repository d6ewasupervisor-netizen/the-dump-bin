Holiday: Mon Sep 7

Team — Labor Day moves things around the week of September 7, and a few stores shift the week before too. Find your store below and read the action line. Each store shows your normal week first, then the two weeks that change. If your store isn't listed under changes, your week runs on the usual schedule.

The rules driving all of it:
  • Monday, September 7 is the holiday — all deliveries, visits, and service are cancelled that day.
  • Some stores that normally write their order later in the week will write it Tuesday, September 8 instead. If that's your store, write the order only on Tuesday 9/8 — do not write it on the visit before. Writing it twice double-orders the store.
  • Changes begin Wednesday, September 2, not the holiday week — watch the dates.
  • Two stores — Federal Way and Auburn — are losing shelf space. This is a separate matter from the holiday schedule, but it lands the same weeks, so it's included here. The remodel team completes these resets — you don't work them; your part is to make sure you're not ordering the deleted items, and the store should have UA tags up. Both delete lists are at the end of this document, organized by store.
Heads up — Tuesday, September 8 is heavy.
Seven stores need a Tuesday 9/8 visit: 19, 23, 111, 215, 391, 658, 682. That's a heavy Tuesday — I need each of you to confirm your September 8 coverage with me before the week starts.
